Why a prior-year cell shows “—” (no number)
  • Per-cell hover — rest the pointer on the em dash in a prior-year column for a short reason tied to that calendar year (including whether adjacent ISO weeks had sales for that product).
  • Not sold that week — usual for off-season or low-volume products; imported 601 ACTUAL rows may simply have no line for this ISO week.
  • That calendar year was not imported — year_YYYY sales tiers outside the import run’s year range are skipped (see preflight and operator import-floor notes). On the host that ran apply, open import_historical_data --summary-json (or <data_dir>/_import_artifacts/historical-import-summary-*.json): compare run.start_year/end_year to run.bundle_disk_year_folder_min/max, and scan results.row_warnings for import_year_range_skips_disk_year_folder.
  • Import left rows unresolved — results.row_errors (channel/product not found) and results.row_warning_code_totals explain hard skips vs warnings; after apply, results.post_repair_planting_events confirms generated harvest rows (LIVE-11 / Tier-1 ops).
  • Channel or product does not line up with history — rows must share the same SalesChannel and Product keys as 601 data; renames, duplicate channels, or id drift can leave cells empty — use staff diagnostics at the top of this page.
  • Product id changed but label stayed the same — reference re-import can replace a CropSalesFormat row; the app can still match older ACTUAL rows by crop + name when the staff panel says so.
  • bunch vs bu on the same crop line — treated as the same sellable step for joins; 12 bu is a different product and will not merge into bu/bunch.
